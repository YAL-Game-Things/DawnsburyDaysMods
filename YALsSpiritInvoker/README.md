# Spirit Invoker

Adds the dwarven archetype, also known as Rivethun Invoker.

Implements only a few things that were of interest to me in a non-FA game:
- Dedication & Spirit Trance
- Invoke Offense
- Invoke Defense

Barbarian/Fighter feats will be included if your game has them
(such as from [Remaster Feats](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3223870401)).
