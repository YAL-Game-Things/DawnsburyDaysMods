# Grasping Reach

Adds [Grasping Reach](https://2e.aonprd.com/Feats.aspx?ID=4493) for leshy! Kind of.
Halfway through implementing the weapon restrictions I thought that -
you know what, I don't think *that* much would break if you could Just Do It.
Sure, specifically turning a 1d10 reach 10ft polearm into a 1d8 reach 15ft is probably strong (maybe don't do that),
but Touch Range reach is already readily available (for Yaoguai or Thlipit Contestants)
and Well-Armed shows that one-handed reach is acceptable as well.

Functionally the effect increases your reach and overrides your melee damage dice to compensate.
