# Blister Bomb

*Are you tired of being nice?*

Implements [Blister Bomb](https://2e.aonprd.com/Spells.aspx?ID=2494), a spell that is widely regarded as *the* way to turn tables in problematic encounters due to inflicting Sickened 2 even on a successful save.

You shouldn't have to resort to this for base-game encounters, but you can see for yourself why it is not allowed in some places.
